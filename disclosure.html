Educational use only

This website is an educational resource for health care professionals. It summarizes a clinical framework to support learning and local implementation planning.

It is not medical advice and does not replace professional judgment. It is not a clinical guideline or protocol in its own right.

No duty of care

No duty of care

Using this website does not create a duty of care, a clinician–patient relationship or any other professional relationship between its authors and any user or patient. The authors do not assess, diagnose or treat anyone through this site.

Every clinical decision remains the responsibility of the treating clinician, working within their scope of practice and their hospital's approved policies, protocols and order sets.

Source of the content

The brief summarizes the Emergency Department Sepsis Clinical Framework (September 2026) and the clinical memorandum of September 29, 2026. Doses, thresholds and time targets are reproduced as written in the framework, and each section header lists the framework pages it draws on.

The framework's publisher retains all rights in the framework. This site is independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the framework's publisher or by any hospital.

The framework states that it does not establish a standard of care or supersede clinical judgment. Where a hospital has approved its own screening tool, pathways, directives or order sets, those take precedence over this summary.

The section "Decisions and gaps to settle locally" contains the authors' own observations from comparing the tools with each other. It is not part of the framework.

Accuracy and currency

Content was last reviewed on September 30, 2026. Guidelines, drug availability and local policies change over time.

Check doses against current references and your hospital's formulary before use. No warranty is given that the content is complete or current, and the authors accept no liability for decisions made using it.

The summary was drafted with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic). Doses, thresholds and time targets were then checked line by line against the source framework.

Access and privacy

Access is limited to invited users because the source framework is intended for authorized health care personnel and is not for public distribution. Please do not copy or share this brief outside that audience.

Sign-in is handled by Cloudflare Access, which uses your email address to send a one-time code and keeps a record of sign-ins for security. The site has no advertising and no analytics or tracking scripts. A small script on the page only remembers your light or dark mode choice in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. Its typefaces load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads.
